Modifications by: Vel, LaTeXTemplates.com

Yi's package removes the optional back-of-book index, replaces the upstream
bibliography plumbing with Pandoc citeproc, and exposes the title, author,
subtitle, copyright and dedication through document metadata.

The background, chapter-heading artwork and illustration are redistributed
from the upstream package under the same licence.
