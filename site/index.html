A writing home

One place for notes, knowledge and finished work.

01

Daily notes

Open a folder, capture a thought and stay in the writing. Files remain readable outside Yi.

  • Quick capture with ordinary Markdown files.
  • Workspace tree, search, session stack and autosave.
  • Light, dark and automatic themes with long-note performance.

02

Personal knowledge base

Connect notes without trapping them in a database. Yi resolves the links while the source stays portable.

  • Wikilinks and automatic cross-references.
  • Structured frontmatter and stable document IDs.
  • CSL citations and local bibliography files.

03

Writing and publishing

Write mathematical and academic prose in the editor, then hand the same note to a trusted template.

  • KaTeX mathematics, callouts, theorem blocks and footnotes.
  • Export to Word, ODT, LaTeX, HTML, EPUB, PDF, Reveal.js and Beamer.
  • Reusable Pandoc template packages and native citation workflows.
